Nexus MD privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Nexus MD does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your documents

Nexus MD opens, edits and saves Markdown files only as files on your device, or in the cloud storage you choose (for example iCloud Drive), using the system's own file access. The app never uploads your documents anywhere and has no server of its own. If you keep a file in iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to it.

Settings stored on your device

The app keeps a few settings on your device, such as your chosen theme, editor options, and the folders you allowed it to read so that images next to a document can be shown. These stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Network

Nexus MD does not make network connections. Diagrams and math are drawn by a hidden web view that loads only files bundled inside the app, and its page blocks every remote load.

Data we collect

None. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties, to sell, or to delete on request.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
